# Outcome-first updates

Tell the reader what changed and what they need to do, not how many agents were busy.

- Lead with the outcome, blocker, or explicit ask. For a small update, one or two sentences may be enough.
- Name the actual artifact and state: proposed, implemented locally, tested, published, reviewed, merged, released, or deployed. These are not synonyms.
- Attribute people only with source-supported roles: owner, reviewer, participant, mentioned person, or unknown. An account name or shared repository does not establish ownership.
- Include the shortest useful evidence link and revision. Explain what the checks prove and what remains untested. Do not inflate a sample into full coverage.
- Make dependencies and the next decision visible. If work is stopped, say so rather than implying background progress.
- Send material completions and actionable blockers promptly; avoid routine narration. Drafting is separate from permission to send, tag, publish, or change PR state.

Use [the worked example](examples/worked.md). Match the recipient and channel rather than mechanically filling a template.
