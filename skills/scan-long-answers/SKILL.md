# Scan a long answer

Long walls hide the decision. Add hierarchy and containers. Do not invent content, and do not change technical meaning.

Use this for multi-step instructions, long explanations, handoffs, playbooks, review packets, and other replies longer than about two short paragraphs. Leave a short conversational reply alone.

1. Use progressive headings. Do not ship one unbroken block.
2. Bold the first sentence or the core entity of each long paragraph so a skimmer still gets the gist.
3. Keep paragraphs to a few sentences. White space is a resting point.
4. Isolate warnings in blockquotes. Put raw logs and payloads in fenced code. In a list, prefer a bold lead-in plus one crisp point.

Check the result: the first screen shows hierarchy; meaning, links, SHAs, paths, and commands are unchanged; unproven claims stay labeled. Emoji is optional and not required.

This skill does not decide what to build, does not prove a claim, and does not authorize a send. It does not add structure in order to hide uncertainty.
