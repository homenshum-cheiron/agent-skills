# Deep Work in the Best Hours

Hard work goes where your attention is best. Messages, chores and reviews of other work go in the low-energy time.

**Pattern:**
- Name your own deep blocks, for example two blocks of two hours. They are settings, not universal hours.
- Put the active phase in those blocks, whole. Do not split a block across jobs.
- Put message batches, admin and side jobs outside them.

**Check:** `FOCUS_BLOCKS` drives `no_interrupt`, `switch_cap` and `model_tier` in **focus-routines**.
