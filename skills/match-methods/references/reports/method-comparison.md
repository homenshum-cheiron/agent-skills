# Method comparison

Use when options, including no change, share criteria. A comparison can be short. Do not add a recommendation that repeats the table.

## Outcome

What choice the reader must make, and the quality floor.

## Current versus desired

Current behavior with its evidence class. Desired behavior as a direction.

## Comparison

One row per candidate, including no change. Columns that apply: mechanism, fit, anti-fit, prerequisites, evidence strength, quality, cost, latency, maintenance, permissions. Mark each cell measured, derived, judged, estimated, or missing. Overlapping benefits are not added. Say so if two candidates share a span.

## Sensitivity

What assumption, if changed, would reorder the rows. If that was not tested, the cell is missing, not zero.

## Visual

Only with a table that matches the sidecar series. Units, denominator, and sample size travel with it. Hypothetical rows are labeled hypothetical.

## Conclusion

Uncertainty, falsifier, and the next authorized step. The conclusion does not grant the step.

## Handoff

Revision and change log.
