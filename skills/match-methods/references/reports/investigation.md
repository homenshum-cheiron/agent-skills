# Investigation

One shape for three kinds: trace diagnosis, experiment result, and negative result. Set the kind in the sidecar. A negative result is a result. Do not hide it.

## Outcome

What is now known, for whom. Name the kind in the first sentence.

## Scope

Current evidence, then desired direction, as separate blocks.

## Method

How the record was produced: frozen revision, clock, what was not recorded, sampling, and export completeness. Denominators sit next to rates.

## Diagnosis or result

Competing causes and the falsifier for the leading one. For an experiment, the baseline identity and date, the single change, and whether quality, evidence, and authority guards held. For a negative result, state the loss and do not drop the change on the score alone. The owner decides.

## Estimates

Assumptions, and whether a combined benefit would double-count a shared span. Estimates are not measurements.

## Visual

See the format note. No numeric chart when the status is hypothesis-only.

## Conclusion

Uncertainty, falsifier, next authorized step. Not permission.

## Handoff

Versioned change log. Candidate, reviewed, landed, deployed, and live stay separate words.
