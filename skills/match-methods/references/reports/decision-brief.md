# Decision brief

Use for a direct answer. Omit empty sections. Pair the page with one JSON sidecar. Do not keep a second set of numbers in prose.

## Outcome

Two or three sentences: the decision, for whom, and what is still not allowed.

## Current evidence and desired direction

Keep these apart. Current is what the record shows. Desired is a direction, not a result.

## Evidence

One table: figure id, class (measured, derived, judged, estimated, or missing), value or null, denominator, sample size or missing. Unknown is not zero.

## Falsifier and next authorized step

What observation would reverse the decision. The next step, and who must authorize it. A recommendation is not that authorization.

## Handoff

Repository or artifact id, revision, and what changed in this version of the note.
