# Start With an If-Then Step

"At 09:30, at the desk, open the failing parser test" gets started. "Work on the parser" waits. Plans phrased as when-then-do are followed more often ([Gollwitzer 1999](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.54.7.493)).

**Pattern:**
- When: a clock time or a cue.
- Where: the place or the tool.
- Action: the first physical step, small enough to do without deciding anything.

**Check:** `done_picture` in **focus-routines** fails when the first step has no time or no action.
