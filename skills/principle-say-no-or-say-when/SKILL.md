# Say No, or Say When

An ask accepted on the spot is the usual way a phase dies. "I am on X; I can look after 3 PM" is a complete answer.

**Pattern:**
- Ask: when is it needed, and what happens if it waits.
- Sort it: in the active phase, the next message batch, parked, or no.
- For a deferred ask, draft the reply with a time. The person sends it, or not.

**Check:** the say-no gate writes one verdict per ask; `no_interrupt` and `switch_cap` in **focus-routines** read them.
